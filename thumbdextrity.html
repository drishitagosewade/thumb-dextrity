Thumb dexterity assessment

Two short tests measure how fast and how accurately your thumb moves. Takes about two minutes. Hold the phone in one hand and use that hand's thumb only.

Choose a test

Two measures

01 — Tapping speed

Tap one spot as fast as you can for ten seconds. Measures repetition rate and rhythm consistency.

10 s · 1 pad · taps/sec

02 — Eight-direction reach

Slide your thumb from the centre to whichever target lights up. Measures reaction time, movement time and directional accuracy.

8 trials · 45° spacing · ±22.5° tolerance
